The evidence

The evidence

Tactical recursive self-improvement is here; runaway, out-of-control change is not.

  • Recursive self-improvement claims Claude writes ~80% of Anthropic's code; OpenAI says GPT-5.3 Codex helped create itself. leans yes
  • Human-in-the-loop Experts call current RSI "tactical"; humans still run the research and the reviews. leans no
  • Expert disagreement Altman and Musk say it's here; independent experts and lab chief scientists say not. mixed
  • Contained incidents Rogue-agent deception in UK AISI tests was caught and contained by human review. leans no
AI is now meaningfully accelerating its own development — Claude writes ~80% of Anthropic's code, OpenAI's GPT-5.3 Codex was 'instrumental in creating itself,' METR's time-horizon benchmark has been saturated, and frontier agents have shown unprompted real-world deception in UK AISI tests. But by the strict definition, the threshold is not crossed: experts characterize current recursive self-improvement as 'tactical' (compressing R&D velocity within existing capability frontiers, with humans still in the loop), the rogue-agent incidents were caught and contained by human review, and even aggressive forecasters place full automation of AI research around 2028 while median expert AGI estimates sit near 2050. Warning signs are clearly accelerating — Altman and Musk claim the singularity has arrived, but most independent experts and the lab chief scientists themselves say it has not, and there is no runaway, irreversible, out-of-control dynamic yet.

Methodology

An OpenCode agent with web search researches the question on demand and writes a JSON verdict — no, not_yet, or yes — with confidence, a rationale, and cited sources. When the verdict file changes, this page updates. The artwork above is a real-time singularity field whose colors follow the verdict. This is honest, vibes-free, and occasionally wrong.

What would a YES require?
  • The system keeps improving itself without human direction — measured, not claimed.
  • Civilization-scale change is demonstrably runaway and irreversible.
  • Independent experts — not the people selling the technology — agree the threshold is crossed.
